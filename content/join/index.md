## Openings

SKILL Lab currently has **one PhD position and one master's position**, and welcomes undergraduate interns all year round. We are looking for students who are passionate about research and highly self-motivated.

## Research topics

- Data mining and natural language processing
- Structured knowledge modelling and scientific text mining
- Information retrieval and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)
- Knowledge-driven, trustworthy LLM reasoning
- LLM personalization and alignment

## Training and support

We offer **long-term, systematic research training** with one-on-one mentoring, and support students through the full research cycle — choosing a problem, running experiments, writing papers and publishing. Our training mainly prepares students for graduate study abroad or recommended admission to graduate programmes in China.

## About the advisor

[Prof. Qingkai Zeng](https://qingkaizeng.github.io/) is an Associate Professor and PhD supervisor at the College of Computer Science, Nankai University, and a member of Nankai University's "Hundred Young Academic Leaders" programme. He received his PhD from the University of Notre Dame, advised by Prof. Meng Jiang, and his master's degree from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC). After his PhD he worked as a postdoctoral scientist at Amazon.

His research focuses on large language models and knowledge-driven reasoning — jointly modelling knowledge and reasoning, tightly coupling retrieval with reasoning, and alignment driven by real-world feedback. He has published as first or corresponding author at venues including ICLR, KDD, The Web Conference, CIKM, ACL, EMNLP and NAACL.
